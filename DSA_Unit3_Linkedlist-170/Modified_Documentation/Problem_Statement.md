#Problem Statement

The Smart Student Management System is implemented in C++ using Singly Linked List, Doubly Linked List, and Circular Linked List to manage student records efficiently. It includes adding, displaying, searching, updating, and deleting student records. It also supports forward and backward navigation, student turn scheduling for presentations and practicals, and academic performance analysis. The system compares the time and space complexities of all three linked lists to demonstrate their efficiency and practical applications.
